Questions

Why do hotel prices drop after I book?

Hotels adjust rates constantly using dynamic pricing software. When occupancy is slower than expected, a group block cancels, or a nearby hotel cuts its rate, the exact same room can drop significantly before your free-cancellation deadline. Most travellers never check again after booking — we watch your reservation for you and alert you if a cheaper rate appears.

How do you make money?

Our booking partner pays us a commission on each rebooking. It's already inside the price you see. [CONFIRM pricing model]

Who takes my payment?

Our booking partner, Nuitée, on a secure hosted checkout. We never store your card. [LEGAL: seller of record]

Do you cancel my original booking?

No. Once the new booking is confirmed, you cancel the original on the site you booked it.

Is the new booking refundable?

We show the cancellation terms before you tap. You confirm the match first.

What if prices never drop?

Nothing happens. You keep your booking and pay nothing extra.
