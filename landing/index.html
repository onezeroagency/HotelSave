Three steps. Then you can forget about it.

  1. Forward your confirmation

    Send your booking email to save@myroomwatch.com from the address you signed up with. We read the hotel, dates, room, board, guests, price and your free-cancellation deadline.

  2. We keep checking

    We look at rates for your hotel and dates on a schedule tied to your deadline: daily at first, then more often in the last two weeks, and every 6 hours in the final 3 days.

  3. You hear from us in time

    Before your deadline closes we email you what we saw. If we've seen lower rates for your dates, we say so and link you to check today's price. If nothing moved, we tell you that too, so you can relax and keep your booking.

The obvious questions.

Which bookings can I forward?

Any hotel booking with free cancellation, from any booking site or the hotel directly, anywhere in the world. Coverage is best where the hotel appears on Google Hotels. Non-refundable bookings can't be rebooked, so we'll tell you if we get one.

Do you change or cancel my booking?

No. We only read the confirmation you forward. Any rebooking or cancelling is yours to do, on the booking site.

How will you tell me about a lower rate?

By email: "We've seen lower rates for your dates", with a link to check today's price. We don't quote a saving we can't stand behind, because prices and cancellation terms differ between sites.

What if nothing changes?

You'll still get a heads-up about 48 hours before your free-cancellation window closes, so you never find out about a deadline too late.

Is rebooking allowed?

Usually, yes. A free-cancellation rate lets you cancel before the deadline, and making a new booking is a normal booking. Always check your booking's own cancellation terms first.

What do you do with my data?

We use it only to watch your booking and email you. Our providers handle hosting, email and reading your confirmation. The full list is in our Privacy policy. Delete your account any time by emailing hello@myroomwatch.com.

What happens after the beta?

We email you before billing starts. Founding members keep €29/year. You can stop at any time.
