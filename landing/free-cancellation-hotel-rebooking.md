# How to rebook a free-cancellation hotel if the rate drops

> Free cancellation is often the reason a hotel rate costs more than the non-refundable alternative. That extra amount only has value if you still have time to use it. The window is a deadline — a date and time on your confirmation — not a general promise that you can change your mind on the day of arrival.

## What the terms actually allow

If the rate is refundable and the deadline has not passed, you can cancel without a fee and you are free to make a new booking. That new booking might be the same hotel, the same dates and a lower public rate. It might also be a different room, a different board, or a rate you cannot later cancel. The original site (Booking.com, Expedia, the hotel, or any other) will not usually re-price the reservation you already hold just because the website now shows a lower number. Replacing it, when the terms allow, is something you do yourself.

## The only safe rebooking order

The safe order is the part most people get wrong. Book the new rate first. Wait until the new confirmation is in your inbox. Then cancel the original. Never cancel first. A price you saw two minutes ago may be gone, and cancelling first can leave you with no reservation at all.

**Book the new rate first, confirm it, then cancel the original. Never cancel first.**

## When it is not worth swapping

Before you swap, compare like with like: same hotel, same dates, a room and board you are actually willing to take, and cancellation terms you understand. If the cheaper offer is non-refundable, only take it if the trip is certain. If the saving is tiny once you have checked taxes, fees, and any difference in breakfast or cancellation rights, keeping the original booking is a perfectly good outcome. If the deadline has passed, stop — the rate you have is the rate you have.

**Is it allowed to cancel a refundable hotel and book it again cheaper?** Usually yes, if you cancel before your own deadline and the new rate is one you are happy with. Always follow the terms on your confirmation.

**Why book the new rate before cancelling?** Because the cheaper rate can disappear. If you cancel first, you may end up with no room, or only a more expensive one.
