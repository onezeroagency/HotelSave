# A hotel price drop tracker for a booking you've already made

> Hotel prices are not frozen the moment you click book. Hotels and booking sites use dynamic pricing, so the public rate for the same room on the same dates can fall — or rise — between the day you reserve and the day you arrive. If you paid extra for free cancellation, that movement is only useful if you notice it while you can still cancel.

## What a post-booking tracker actually does

Most people do not. Once the confirmation email arrives, the booking feels finished. Rechecking the same hotel, on the same dates, every few days until the cancellation window closes is tedious, and almost nobody keeps it up.

That is the job of a post-booking hotel price drop tracker: it watches a stay you already hold, not a trip you have not booked yet.

## How MyRoomWatch works

MyRoomWatch is built for that moment. You create an account with your email only — no password, and no card for the free plan — and forward your refundable hotel confirmation to save@myroomwatch.com. We read the hotel, dates, room, board, guests, price and your free-cancellation deadline. We then keep checking public rates for that same stay on a schedule tied to your deadline: daily at first, more often in the last two weeks, and every six hours in the final three days. Coverage is best where the hotel appears on Google Hotels. Confirmations can come from any booking site, including Booking.com or Expedia, or from the hotel directly.

About 48 hours before your free-cancellation window closes, we email you what we saw. If we have seen lower rates for your dates, we say so and give you a link to check today's price. If nothing moved, we tell you that too, so you can keep your booking with a clear mind.

A few things we deliberately do not do. We do not send an email the instant a number twitches. We do not quote a saving we cannot stand behind, because prices and cancellation terms differ between sites and can change between our check and your click. We do not touch your reservation: we cannot change or cancel it, and we never book anything in your name.

If you decide the new rate is better, the safe order is always: book the new rate first, confirm it, then cancel the original on the original site. Never cancel first. Near the deadline, a non-refundable rate for the same room is sometimes cheaper — only switch if you are sure you are going.

## Who it is for

This is for travellers who already hold a free-cancellation booking and do not want the deadline to pass quietly. It is not useful for a non-refundable rate, and it is not a promise that every stay will get cheaper. Plenty of bookings never drop. The value is that you find out in time either way.

## Questions

**What is a hotel price drop tracker?** A tool that rechecks the rate for a hotel stay — ideally one you have already booked — and tells you if a lower public price appears. MyRoomWatch does this for refundable bookings and emails you about 48 hours before your free-cancellation deadline.

**Do I need to have booked already?** That is the case MyRoomWatch is built for. Forward a confirmation you already have. Non-refundable bookings cannot be replaced this way.

**Will you rebook it for me?** No. We only read the confirmation and email you. Any cancel-and-rebook is yours to do on the booking site.

**Do you send instant alerts?** No. We check on a schedule that gets more frequent as your deadline nears, and we email you about 48 hours before the window closes.

**Does it work worldwide?** Yes, anywhere we can identify the hotel and dates. Coverage is best where the hotel appears on Google Hotels.
