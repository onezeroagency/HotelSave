Booking.com price dropped after I booked. Here's what that actually means.

It is a familiar inbox surprise: you booked a hotel on Booking.com, and a few days later the same room on the same dates is listed for less than you paid. Sometimes it is a small dip. Sometimes it is enough to notice. The useful question is not “is this allowed?” — it is “is my rate still cancellable, and do I still have time?”

When you can actually do something about it

If you booked a free-cancellation rate, Booking.com's own terms for that reservation usually let you cancel up to a stated deadline (check the exact date, time, and time zone on your confirmation, not on a generic help article). Cancelling a refundable booking and making a new one is a normal booking, not a loophole. If you booked a non-refundable rate, a later drop is almost always something you have to live with.

Before you do anything, open the confirmation and check three things: the cancellation deadline, whether the new listing is the same stay (same hotel, dates, room type, board and guest count), and whether the new rate's own cancellation terms are acceptable. Member, Genius, or promotional rates can look different from the public price you see when you are logged out — always confirm the live total on Booking.com before you act.

The safe cancel-and-rebook order

If you do decide to replace the booking, use this order only: book the new rate first, wait until the new confirmation is in your inbox, then cancel the original. Never cancel first. A rate can disappear between two page loads, and you do not want to be left with no room.

Questions

Can I cancel a Booking.com booking and book it again cheaper?

If the reservation is still free to cancel, usually yes. Read the cancellation terms on your confirmation first, including the deadline.

Does MyRoomWatch work with Booking.com confirmations?

Yes. Forward the confirmation from the email you signed up with. We are not affiliated with Booking.com.

Will you change my Booking.com reservation?

No. We only email you. You book and cancel yourself on Booking.com.

What if the cheaper rate is non-refundable?

Only switch if you are certain you will take the trip. A cheaper non-refundable rate is a different product from the free-cancellation one you may already hold.

What if I booked through the hotel or another site?

That is fine — see the hotel price drop tracker guide. The same watch flow applies.
