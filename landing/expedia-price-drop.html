Expedia price dropped after I booked. Here's what to do next.

You booked a hotel on Expedia, and now the same stay is showing a lower price. That can happen. Expedia, like other booking sites, sells rates that move with demand, occupancy and timing. A lower number on the hotel page does not automatically change the reservation you already have. Whether you can do anything depends on the cancellation terms attached to your booking.

Check your own cancellation terms first

If the rate was refundable — free cancellation up to a deadline stated on your Expedia confirmation — you can usually cancel before that deadline and make a new booking at the price you see today. If the rate was non-refundable, or the deadline has passed, a later drop generally cannot be claimed. Package bookings that combine a hotel with a flight or car can also have different rules from a hotel-only rate. Read the cancellation section on your confirmation, including the date, time and time zone, before you act.

Match the new offer to the stay you already hold: same hotel, same dates, comparable room and board, same number of guests. Then look at the new rate's own cancellation policy. A cheaper price that you cannot cancel may or may not be a good swap.

The safe cancel-and-rebook order

If you decide to replace the booking, book the new rate first, confirm that the new reservation exists, and only then cancel the original on Expedia. Never cancel first. Inventory can vanish in the gap between two checks.

Questions

Can I cancel an Expedia hotel booking and book again at a lower rate?

If your booking is still free to cancel, usually yes. Confirm the deadline and terms on your Expedia confirmation first.

Does MyRoomWatch work with Expedia confirmations?

Yes. Forward the confirmation from the email address you signed up with. We are not affiliated with Expedia.

Do you rebook the Expedia reservation for me?

No. You book and cancel yourself on Expedia. We only watch the rate and email you before the deadline.

What about a hotel + flight package?

Package terms can differ from hotel-only rates. Read your confirmation carefully; if the hotel portion is not freely cancellable, this pattern may not apply.
