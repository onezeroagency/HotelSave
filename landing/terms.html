1. What the service does

You forward a hotel booking confirmation to us. We identify the property and stay, then periodically check whether the same stay — same hotel, dates, occupancy and board, still refundable — is available at a lower price. If it is, and the difference is meaningful, we email you before your free-cancellation window closes. We also tell you when that window is about to close even if we found nothing.

2. What the service does not do

  • We never make, change or cancel a booking for you. Every rebooking is yours to make, directly with the hotel or booking site.
  • We never take payment details for a hotel stay and never hold your card.
  • We are not a travel agent, not a booking platform, and not a party to your reservation.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Only forward bookings that are yours, and give us accurate details.
  • Check the rate yourself before you act. Prices and availability change constantly; confirm the new rate, its terms and its cancellation policy on the provider's own site before booking.
  • Book the new rate first, confirm it, then cancel the original. Never cancel first — you could lose the room and end up worse off. We are not responsible for a booking cancelled before a replacement was secured.
  • Verify your own cancellation deadline with the provider. We work from what the confirmation says; if it's wrong or ambiguous, so are we.
  • Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for activity on your account.
